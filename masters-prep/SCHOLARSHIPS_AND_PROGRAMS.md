# Fully Funded Scholarships: Cybersecurity & Cloud Security Master's 🛡️☁️💰

This guide details **fully funded scholarship programs** that specifically support **coursework / taught Master's degrees in Cybersecurity, Information Security, and Cloud Infrastructure Security** (designed for practical systems defense, penetration testing, security architecture, and founding a cybersecurity firm or landing high-paying security roles).

---

## 🌟 Tier 1: Flagship Cybersecurity & Cloud Master's

### 1. Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program @ Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) Africa
- **Location:** Kigali, Rwanda (Direct American degree from Carnegie Mellon University, USA — global #1 in Computer & Cyber Security Engineering)
- **Program:** Master of Science in Information Technology (MSIT) — **Cybersecurity & Applied Cloud Concentration**
- **Coursework Focus:** 100% applied: Network Defense, Cryptography in Software Systems, Cloud Architecture, Hardware Security, and Threat Intelligence. Includes a mandatory summer industry internship at leading tech/security enterprises.
- **Coverage:** 100% Full ride — tuition fees, monthly living allowance, on-campus housing, comprehensive health insurance, round-trip flights, and a brand-new professional laptop.
- **Why this fits your goal:** CMU is an NSA/CISA National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense. Graduates either join top multinational tech firms (AWS, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare) or establish leading cybersecurity/cloud infrastructure consultancies across Africa.
- **Application Window:** September – December (Annually)
- **Website:** [cmu.edu/africa/admissions](https://www.cmu.edu/africa/admissions/index.html)

---

### 2. UK Government Chevening Scholarships (United Kingdom)
- **Location:** Leading National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) Certified UK Universities
- **Program Type:** 1-Year Taught Master of Science (MSc)
- **Top NCSC-Certified Taught Target Programs:**
  - **University College London (UCL):** *MSc Information Security* (World-leading security lab, covers network security, crypto systems, security operations, and digital forensics).
  - **University of Edinburgh:** *MSc Cyber Security, Privacy and Trust* (Coursework covering secure programming, computer architecture defense, and cloud systems).
  - **University of Southampton:** *MSc Cyber Security* (NCSC Certified Center of Excellence in Cyber Security Research & Teaching).
  - **University of Warwick:** *MSc Cyber Security Engineering* (Applied industrial security).
  - **University of Birmingham:** *MSc Cyber Security* (NCSC Certified).
- **Coverage:** 100% full tuition, monthly living stipend (approx. £1,300–£1,600/month), return flights, arrival/departure allowances, and UK visa fees.
- **Selection Criteria:** Prioritizes candidates with clear **industry leadership and venture-building vision** over theoretical paper-writers.
- **Application Window:** Early August – Early November (Strict annual deadline)
- **Website:** [chevening.org](https://www.chevening.org/)

### 3. Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Cybersecurity (CYBERUS) (European Union)
- **Location:** Study in France (Université de Bretagne Sud) and Estonia (University of Tartu / Tallinn University of Technology - home of the NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence!)
- **Program Type:** 2-Year Taught Joint MSc with Compulsory Industry Security Internships
- **Specialization Tracks:**
  - *IoT Cybersecurity:* Hardware security, embedded systems, physical layer defense, MQTT/network protocol hardening.
  - *Software Cybersecurity:* Secure software architecture, cloud security, penetration testing, malware analysis, and DevSecOps.
- **Coverage:** Full tuition waiver, **€1,400 per month** living allowance, €3,000/year travel & installation grant, and complete health insurance across Europe.
- **Application Window:** October – January / February
- **Website:** [master-cyberus.eu](https://master-cyberus.eu/)

---

### 4. DAAD Scholarships & Public Universities (Germany)
- **Location:** Germany (World-class cybersecurity hubs)
- **Target Institutions:**
  - **Saarland University / CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security:** *MSc Cybersecurity* (CISPA is one of the top cyber security research and industrial defense centers worldwide).
  - **TU Darmstadt:** *MSc IT Security / Cybersecurity* (Host of ATHENE, the largest research center for applied cybersecurity in Europe).
  - **Ruhr University Bochum:** *MSc IT Security / Networks & Systems* (Horst Görtz Institute for IT Security).
- **Funding & Benefits:**
  - Tuition is **0 EUR** at public universities.
  - **DAAD Scholarships** provide **€934/month** stipend + health insurance + travel costs.
  - Germany has an **18-month post-study job search visa** and a massive shortage of cybersecurity and cloud security specialists. High demand for security consulting firms and enterprise defense engineers.
- **Application Window:** September – November (DAAD portal) / January – May (direct university portal)
- **Website:** [daad.de/en](https://www.daad.de/en/)

---

### 5. Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SISGP) (Sweden)
- **Location:** Sweden (KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Chalmers, Stockholm University)
- **Program Type:** 2-Year Taught Master's in *Cybersecurity* or *Information and Network Security*
- **Coverage:** 100% full tuition covered directly, **12,000 SEK/month** living stipend, 15,000 SEK travel grant, and comprehensive insurance.
- **Focus:** Building leadership and tech entrepreneurship for global professionals.
- **Application Window:** University Admissions (October – January); SISGP Scholarship portal (February)
- **Website:** [si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/)

---

### 6. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships (United Kingdom)
- **Location:** Partner universities in the UK (e.g., Birmingham, Cranfield, Sheffield)
- **Program Type:** 1-Year Taught Master's in *Cyber Security* or *Cloud Computing Security*
- **Coverage:** Full tuition fees, approved return airfare, monthly living allowance (£1,347–£1,652/month).
- **Eligibility:** Citizens of Commonwealth developing countries (Ghana qualifies). Designed for practical technological and economic resilience.
- **Application Window:** November – December
- **Website:** [cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/shared-scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/shared-scholarships/)
