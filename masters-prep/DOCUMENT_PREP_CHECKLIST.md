# Master's Application Document Checklist 📁✅

This checklist details every essential document needed for graduate school applications and fully funded scholarship submissions.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Academic Credentials

- [ ] **Official UMaT Academic Transcript**
  - Obtain an official, stamped student transcript from the UMaT Academic Affairs / Examinations Office reflecting your current completed semesters (CWA: 80.87, First Class standing).
  - Also retain a high-resolution scanned digital PDF copy for direct portal upload.
- [ ] **English as Medium of Instruction (MOI) Proficiency Letter**
  - **Crucial Money Saver:** Request an official letter from UMaT's Academic Registry stating that the medium of instruction for all lectures, labs, and examinations in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering is English.
  - *Why it matters:* Most universities in the UK, Europe, and Canada accept this letter to **completely waive the $200–$250 IELTS/TOEFL test requirement** for Ghanaian university graduates.
- [ ] **WASSCE Certificate / Result Slip**
  - A clean, certified scan of your West African Senior School Certificate Examination results (Kumasi Academy).
- [ ] **Degree Certificate / Expected Completion Letter**
  - If applying before final graduation, obtain an official "Attestation of Studentship & Expected Completion Date" letter from UMaT.

## ✍️ 3. Letters of Recommendation (Referees)

*Most scholarships require 2 (or occasionally 3) recommendation letters.*

- [ ] **Referee 1: Academic Recommendation (UMaT CSE Department)**
  - Target: A senior lecturer or professor from UMaT who taught you in high-scoring courses (e.g., Software Engineering, Operating Systems, or Database Systems).
  - Focus: Intellectual capability, analytical rigor, academic consistency (80.87 CWA), and lab problem-solving.
- [ ] **Referee 2: Professional / Industry Recommendation (AmaliTech / Tech Leadership)**
  - Target: Program manager, technical trainer, or mentor from the AmaliTech AWS re/Start program or coding club.
  - Focus: Real-world engineering execution, cloud proficiency, work ethic, teamwork during hackathons, and leadership in peer mentoring.
- [ ] **Referee Preparation Kit:**
  - Provide your referees with:
    1. Your updated CV.
    2. Your draft Statement of Purpose.
    3. A bulleted summary of your contributions under their supervision.
    4. Exact submission deadlines and portal link instructions.
