### Motivation Letter Draft:

Dear Members of the CYBERUS Academic Selection Consortium,

I am writing to express my enthusiastic application for admission and the prestigious Erasmus Mundus scholarship for the **CYBERUS Joint Master in Cybersecurity (Software Cybersecurity Track)**. As an applied Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the University of Mines and Technology (UMaT), Ghana, my ultimate career objective is to operate as an elite cloud security solutions architect and establish a premier Managed Security Services Provider (MSSP) and cyber defense consultancy in West Africa. The international curriculum, operational defense labs, and cross-border mobility of the CYBERUS program represent the definitive educational environment required to achieve this goal.

Throughout my undergraduate studies at UMaT, I maintained a relentless focus on practical systems engineering, achieving a **First Class Honours standing with an 80.87 Cumulative Weighted Average (CWA)**. Through rigorous academic coursework in *Operating Systems, Computer Networks, Database Systems, and Cryptography*, I developed an intuitive understanding of low-level vulnerability vectors and network communication architectures. To bridge theory with defensive practice, I designed an academic research proposal for a multi-layer campus network infrastructure incorporating IEEE 802.1X authentication, DHCP snooping, and Layer 3 VRF-lite segmentation.

Recognizing that modern infrastructure has fundamentally shifted to cloud environments, I achieved the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CCP)** credential following the intensive AWS re/Start cloud program with AmaliTech, mastering Linux administration, virtual private cloud (VPC) segmentation, and IAM least-privilege security policies. Concurrently, I earned the **Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** from ISC2 and participate actively in the **UMaT Cybersecurity Club**, executing network host enumeration with Nmap and mitigating SQL injection vulnerabilities on experimental databases.

My ability to build and deliver secure software is evidenced by **VoteEQ** ([voteeq.online](https://voteeq.online)), a live voting and ticketing platform adopted by the UMaT Computer Science and Engineering Students Association. Because the platform processed real financial settlements, I integrated secure Paystack webhook verification, end-to-end HTTPS encryption, and WebSocket live updates, hosting the backend on hardened AWS EC2 instances. Furthermore, as the **AmaliTech Coding Club Organizer**, I mentored over 100 student developers, instilling rigorous defensive programming standards and secure coding hygiene.

The CYBERUS Master program is unmatched because of its synergy between French systems engineering rigor at Université de Bretagne Sud and the cutting-edge cyber defense ecosystem of Estonia (University of Tartu / Tallinn), home of the NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence. The Software Cybersecurity Track’s coursework in *Cloud and Virtualization Security, Secure Software Engineering, Penetration Testing, and Malware Analysis* provides the exact hands-on, coursework-intensive training I require. Unlike purely theoretical programs, CYBERUS’s direct integration with European cybersecurity industrial internships will give me invaluable exposure to global security operations centers (SOCs) and incident handling methodologies.

Following graduation, I intend to work as a **Senior Cloud Security Engineer** before returning to Ghana to establish a specialized **Cyber Defense Consultancy & MSSP**. As West African digital banking and public cloud systems expand, indigenous enterprises are in desperate need of affordable, high-grade threat monitoring and penetration auditing. Through CYBERUS and the Erasmus Mundus scholarship, I will acquire the world-class European defensive engineering standards necessary to defend critical digital infrastructure and champion cyber resilience across Africa.

Thank you for your consideration of my application.
