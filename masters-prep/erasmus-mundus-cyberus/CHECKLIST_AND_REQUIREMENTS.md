# Erasmus Mundus: CYBERUS (Joint Master in Cybersecurity) 🇪🇺🛡️

**Program Name:** CYBERUS — Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree in Cybersecurity  
**Consortium Universities:**
1. Université de Bretagne Sud (France) — Semester 1 & 4
2. Université du Luxembourg (Luxembourg) or University of Tartu / Tallinn University of Technology (Estonia) — Semester 2 & 3
**Scholarship:** Erasmus+ EMJM Scholarship (100% Fully Funded)  
**Financial Award:** **€1,400 per month** living allowance for 24 months + 100% tuition waiver + €3,000/year travel & installation grant + full European health insurance.  
**Annual Window:** October – January / February  
**Application Portal:** [master-cyberus.eu](https://master-cyberus.eu/)

---

## 🎯 Specialization Tracks
You will choose between two elite tracks:
1. **Software Cybersecurity Track:** Focuses on secure software architecture, cloud and virtualization security, malware reverse engineering, and formal methods. *(Recommended for your software and cloud background!)*
2. **IoT Cybersecurity Track:** Focuses on physical systems defense, smart devices, hardware vulnerabilities, and sensor network protocol security.
