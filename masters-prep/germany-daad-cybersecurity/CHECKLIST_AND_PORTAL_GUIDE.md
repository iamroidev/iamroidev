# Germany DAAD Scholarships — Master's in Cybersecurity 🇩🇪🛡️

**Program Focus:** MSc in Cybersecurity / Applied IT Security  
**Key Target Universities:**
1. **Saarland University / CISPA** (Helmholtz Center for Information Security) — Master of Science in Cybersecurity
2. **TU Darmstadt** — Master of Science in IT Security (Host of ATHENE, Europe's largest applied cybersecurity center)
3. **Ruhr University Bochum** — MSc IT Security (Horst Görtz Institute)
**Funding Mechanism:**
- **Tuition:** **0 EUR** (Free at German public universities).
- **DAAD Scholarship:** **€934 per month** living allowance, plus health, accident, and personal liability insurance, travel allowance, and study allowance.
- **Post-Study Advantage:** **18-month job search visa** post-graduation with straightforward pathway to work as a security engineer or launch a German tech firm (GmbH).
**Annual Application Window:**
- DAAD Scholarship Portal: August – October 31 (Annually)
- Direct University Admission Portals: January – May (for Winter semester intake starting October)
