# Commonwealth Shared Scholarships — Cybersecurity 🇬🇧🛡️

**Award:** Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC)  
**Degree Type:** 1-Year Taught Master of Science (MSc) — 100% Fully Funded  
**Target Focus:** Science and Technology for Development (MSc Cyber Security)  
**Partner Universities:** University of Birmingham, Cranfield University, University of Sheffield  
**Annual Window:** Mid-November – Mid-December (Strict annual deadline)  
**Application Portal:** [cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/shared-scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/apply/shared-scholarships/)

---

## 💰 Funding Package
- Full approved university tuition fees.
- Monthly living allowance (£1,347/month outside London; £1,652/month inside London).
- Approved return airfare between Ghana and the United Kingdom.
- Warm clothing allowance upon arrival.
- Thesis/study travel grant.
