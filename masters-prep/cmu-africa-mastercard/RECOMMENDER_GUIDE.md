# CMU Africa Recommender Guidance & Briefing Kit ✉️

CMU Africa requires **three (3) letters of recommendation**. Use this guide to brief your referees so their letters strongly reinforce your technical competence, leadership, and suitability for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program.
