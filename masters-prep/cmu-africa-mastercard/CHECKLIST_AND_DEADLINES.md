# CMU Africa — Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program 🇷🇼🇺🇸

**University:** Carnegie Mellon University Africa (Kigali, Rwanda)  
**Degree:** Master of Science in Information Technology (MSIT)  
**Concentration:** Cybersecurity & Applied Cloud Engineering  
**Scholarship:** Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program (100% Fully Funded)  
**Annual Window:** September – January 15 (Early consideration: December 15)  
**Application Portal:** [CMU Africa Admissions Portal](https://www.cmu.edu/africa/admissions/how-to-apply.html)

---

## 💰 Scholarship Benefits
- 100% full tuition waiver for 2 years (direct CMU Pittsburgh curriculum & degree).
- Monthly living stipend covering accommodation, food, and daily expenses.
- Brand new enterprise-grade development laptop.
- Comprehensive health insurance.
- Round-trip airfare from Ghana to Kigali and return.
- Full funding for mandatory summer industry practicum/internship.
