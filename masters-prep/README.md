# Masters & Fully Funded Scholarship Hub 🎓🛡️☁️

Welcome to the **Master's Preparation & Scholarship Tracker**. This workspace is tailored specifically for **taught/coursework-based Master of Science (MSc) programs** with **100% fully funded scholarships**, focused on:
1. **Cybersecurity / Information Security (Primary Focus)**
2. **Cloud Engineering & Cloud Security (Secondary Focus)**
3. **Software Systems & Architecture (Third Focus)**

The ultimate objective is **Industry Leadership & Enterprise Founding** — acquiring advanced practical defense engineering capabilities to either lead enterprise cloud security teams or establish a specialized **Managed Security Services Provider (MSSP) and Cyber Defense Consultancy in Ghana/West Africa** (rather than academic/thesis paper-writing).
