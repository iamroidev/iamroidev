# Sweden CV Format Guide: Swedish Institute (SISGP) 🇸🇪📄

Applying for a Master's degree in Sweden and the **Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP)** involves **the strictest CV format rules in the world**.

Failing to follow these specific instructions results in **automatic disqualification without review**.

---

## ⚠️ The Non-Negotiable Swedish Institute Rules

| Rule | Requirement | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Strict Mandatory Template** | You **MUST** use the official Swedish Institute CV template (downloadable from `si.se` each January). Any applicant submitting an ad-hoc CV or Europass CV is disqualified immediately. | Swedish reviewers screen thousands of applications using standardized scoring criteria. |
| **2. Maximum 3 Pages** | The completed CV must not exceed **3 pages**. Any page beyond page 3 is deleted before the reviewer sees it. | Conciseness and strict adherence to guidelines are tested. |
| **3. NO PHOTO AT ALL** | **Do NOT include a photograph.** Unlike Germany, Sweden strictly enforces merit-based, blind recruitment standards to prevent unconscious demographic/racial bias. | Including a photo can violate Swedish anti-discrimination screening rules. |
| **4. Align with Work Forms** | The roles, companies, and dates listed in your CV's Work Experience section must **match 100%** with the details on your official *Proof of Work Experience Form* (demonstrating 3,000+ hours). | Inconsistencies between your CV and work forms cause immediate disqualification for fraud or carelessness. |
| **5. No Reference Section** | Do not list referees on your CV. The Swedish Institute has two separate, dedicated *Letters of Reference* forms that must be filled, signed, and stamped by your referees. | Saves space for your 3-page limit. |

---

## 📂 Swedish Institute CV Structure
The official SI CV template is divided into five numbered sections:
1. **Personal Information:** Full Name, Date of Birth, Citizenship, University Admissions Application Number.
2. **Education:** Higher education degrees (UMaT), dates, GPA/standing.
3. **Work Experience:** Up to 3 positions proving 3,000+ hours (AmaliTech intern, freelance/contract engineer, VoteEQ lead).
4. **Leadership Experience:** Specific leadership posts (AmaliTech Coding Club Organizer, UMaT Cybersecurity Club).
5. **Community Engagement & Extracurriculars:** Peer tutoring, open-source projects, developer networks.
6. **Certifications & Skills:** ISC2 CC, AWS CCP, Stanford ML, technical skills.
