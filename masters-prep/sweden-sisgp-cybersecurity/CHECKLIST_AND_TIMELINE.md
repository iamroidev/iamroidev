# Swedish Institute Scholarship (SISGP) — Cybersecurity Master's 🇸🇪🛡️

**Award:** Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals (SISGP)  
**Target Degree:** Master of Science in Cybersecurity / Information & Network Security  
**Primary Institution:** KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Stockholm, Sweden)  
**Financial Award:**  
- 100% full university tuition fee covered directly to KTH.  
- **12,000 SEK per month** living allowance for the full 2 years.  
- 15,000 SEK travel grant.  
- Full Swedish state health and injury insurance.  
**Annual Window:**  
- Step 1: University Admissions Sweden portal (Mid-October – Mid-January)  
- Step 2: Swedish Institute Scholarship portal (Mid-February, open for ~10 days)  
**Portals:**  
- [universityadmissions.se](https://www.universityadmissions.se/) (Course Application)  
- [si.se](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/) (Scholarship Application)
