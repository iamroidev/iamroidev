# Swedish Institute (SISGP) Work & Leadership Experience Guide ⏱️

The Swedish Institute requires applicants to certify a minimum of **3,000 hours of demonstrated work experience** (equivalent to approx. 1.5–2 years of full-time or part-time work, internships, freelance, or student leadership).

---

## 📊 How to Calculate and Document Your 3,000 Hours

The SI allows you to combine up to **three (3) different organizations/employers** to reach the 3,000 hours. All hours must be certified using the official SI PDF form by a supervisor or manager.

## 📝 Key Rules for the SI Forms
1. **Use Only the Official Template:** The Swedish Institute releases its exact PDF templates in January every year. Do NOT use any other document format.
2. **Include Official Stamps & Signatures:** Every form must be stamped with the official stamp/seal of the organization and signed by the referee.
3. **Keep Descriptions Impact-Focused:** Emphasize how your work contributed to digital access, cloud migration, security education, or system efficiency.
