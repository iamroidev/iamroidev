## 📌 Status Legend
- `Researching`: Checking specific curriculum modules, scholarship eligibility, and exact deadlines.
- `Shortlisted`: Decided to apply; preparing profile materials.
- `Drafting SOP`: Writing and tailoring statement of purpose / essays.
- `Recommenders Contacted`: Lecturers and managers notified to write letters.
- `Submitted`: Application lodged and confirmation number received.
- `Interview`: Invited for scholarship interview.
- `Offer`: Admission / Fully funded scholarship awarded!
