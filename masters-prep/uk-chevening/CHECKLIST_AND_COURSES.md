# UK Chevening Scholarship — Cybersecurity & Cloud Master's 🇬🇧🛡️

**Award:** UK Government Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO)  
**Degree Type:** 1-Year Taught Master of Science (MSc) — 100% Fully Funded  
**Application Window:** Early August – Early November (Strict annual deadline)  
**Application Portal:** [chevening.org/apply](https://www.chevening.org/apply/)

---

## 💰 Chevening Financial Coverage
- 100% university tuition fee covered directly to the UK university.
- Monthly living stipend (£1,347/month outside London; £1,652/month in London).
- Return economy class airfare between Ghana and the United Kingdom.
- UK Student Visa application fee reimbursement.
- Arrival allowance and departure allowance.

---

## 🏛️ Top 3 Chosen UK Master's Courses (Required by Chevening)

Chevening requires you to choose **three similar master's courses** across UK universities. All 3 chosen programs must be **National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) Certified** taught degrees:

1. **Course 1 (First Choice):**  
   - **University:** University College London (UCL)  
   - **Program:** MSc Information Security  
   - **Modules:** Network Security, Applied Cryptography, Security Operations & Incident Response, Computer Security Verification.

2. **Course 2:**  
   - **University:** University of Edinburgh  
   - **Program:** MSc Cyber Security, Privacy and Trust  
   - **Modules:** Secure Programming, Computer Architecture Defense, Cloud Security & Trust, Human Factors in Security.

3. **Course 3:**  
   - **University:** University of Southampton  
   - **Program:** MSc Cyber Security  
   - **Modules:** Secure Cloud Architecture, Cryptography Engineering, Penetration Testing & Defense, Cyber Crime & Law.
